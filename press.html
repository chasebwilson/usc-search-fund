Press & events

Where the ecosystem gathers.

Search fund conferences are where searchers meet investors, deals get sourced, and the people in this asset class find each other. Going before you have a fund is the cheapest way to find out whether you want one.

The calendar

The ETA conference circuit.

Dates move year to year, so each entry links to the host, not to a single year’s registration page. Check the host site for the current edition.

SoCal ETA Conference April · Los Angeles
Co-hosted by USC Marshall and UCLA Anderson. Southern California’s flagship ETA gathering, run jointly by the USC Marshall Entrepreneur Association and the UCLA Anderson ETA Club. Two tracks, one for first-time searchers and one for operators. This is the one closest to home — and the one USC helps put on.
Booth–Kellogg ETA Conference November · Chicago
The largest ETA conference in the country, drawing north of 900 attendees. Hosted by the Chicago Booth ETA Group with the Polsky Center and Kellogg. Heavy presence from lenders, brokers, and deal attorneys alongside searchers and investors.
HBS Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition Conference Fall · Boston
Harvard Business School’s annual ETA conference, run out of the Rock Center for Entrepreneurship. HBS also runs a session series through the academic year featuring search investors and alumni who have bought companies.
Wharton ETA Summit Spring · Philadelphia
Held at Huntsman Hall. Denser on institutional investors than most, which makes it a useful room if you are raising rather than sourcing.
Southeast ETA Conference (SEETA) September · rotating host
Co-hosted by Georgetown McDonough, Duke Fuqua, UNC Kenan-Flagler, and UVA Darden. The host school rotates year to year, so the location changes — check the site before booking travel.

Know of one we are missing? Tell us.

In the press

What the business press is writing.

Search funds have gone from a footnote to a recurring subject in the business press, driven by the same demographic story that makes the model work. A selection is collected here.

Several of these sit behind a paywall. Most are readable free through the USC Libraries with your student login.

This Shortcut to Private-Equity Riches Is Minting Young Millionaires The Wall Street Journal · 2026
The most current mainstream account of the boom — search funds, independent sponsors, and SBA-financed acquisitions, with the personal risk that comes with them.
MBA Hotshots Just Want a One-Off Private Equity Deal Financial Times · 2024
A clear-eyed overview of the traditional search fund model and why it has become the destination of choice for ambitious MBAs.
Search Funds: An MBA Shortcut to the C-Suite Bloomberg Businessweek · 2012
Follows Chris Lueck — now an adjunct professor in this program — raising Pylon Capital straight out of USC Marshall. The closest thing this list has to a home story.
Paying Entrepreneurs to Find the Right Business The New York Times · 2009
One of the earliest major-media treatments of the model, from a time when a few dozen search funds existed rather than several hundred.
Offering an Exit Strategy for Aging European Business Owners Fortune · 2015
The succession side of the trade. Follows the first search fund acquisition in Spain and the aging-owner problem driving the model’s spread in Europe.
How CEOs-in-Waiting Buy the Companies They Want to Run Fast Company · 2018
Search funds as a route to the corner office for people with neither a startup idea nor the résumé to be hired as CEO. Draws on Stanford research.
How Entrepreneurial MBA Grads Can Benefit From Search Funds Fortune · 2022
The mechanics: Stanford return data, searcher economics, and the MBA-to-CEO path, with the growth curve from 11 funds in 2010 to 66 in 2020.
Search Funds: A Financing Option for Business Buyers Entrepreneur · 2011
A plain explanation of the two-stage model — money to search, then money to buy — told through Jim Southern and Pacific Lake Partners.
Acquiring Companies Without Money: The Rise of Fundless Search Models Forbes · 2014
Brent Beshore on the difference between a traditional search fund and a fundless sponsor, and why both compete well in the lower middle market.
How the Search Fund Model Is Reshaping Small Business Ownership Forbes · 2026
A current read on search funds as a bridge between retiring founders and the next generation of owners.
The Small Business Acquisition Boom Is Creating a New Class of Entrepreneurs Forbes · 2026
Buying rather than founding as a mainstream career path, set against roughly six million businesses expected to change hands by 2035.
These Moms Ditched Corporate Life to Buy Their Own Businesses Fast Company · 2026
More than ten women who left corporate roles to acquire companies, spanning self-funded, traditional, incubated, and sponsored search.
